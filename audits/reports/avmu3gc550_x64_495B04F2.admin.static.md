## 5 Memory-write trace

memcpy call sites in the dump (about 40) were grepped. Sites checked in this pass:

1. **FUN_00022D24 (dump 13472-13521), memcpy at 13514:** `puVar1 = ExAllocatePoolWithTag(iVar2 = param_3 + 4)`; `memcpy(puVar1 + 4, param_4, param_3)` (dump 13514). Destination size and length match exactly (4 + param_3). Bounded. The source length is a byte (param_3 <= 255).
2. **memcpy at 13520 (`memcpy(param_6, _Src, _Size)`, `_Size = param_5`):** `param_6` is a caller-supplied output pointer. Whether the KS/caller layer validates it was not traced (see §8).
3. **Dump 9248-9265:** for each table entry, `ExAllocatePoolWithTag(uVar6)` followed by `memcpy(alloc, _Src, uVar6)` (9264). The size and the copy length are the same value uVar6, read from the table entry (`*_Src` at dump 9250). Bounded by construction.
4. **Dump 12263-12312 (memcpy with `uVar3` / `uVar5` lengths into `_Dst` and `param_2`):** not traced in this pass (§8).
5. **Other memcpy sites** (3752, 4886-4888, 5057-5076, 5159-5176, 5283, 5364, 5412-5414, 6701, 6775, 7598, 7865, 10981, 11636, 11713, 11907, 13395, 13589, 13649, 13727, 14180): fixed-size copies of structures (0x14, 0x20, 0x2C, 0x40, 0x58 bytes) or length-bounded copies not traced individually.

Conclusion of this pass: no site was found that writes to a caller-chosen kernel address. The checked sites are sized from the same value used to allocate, or are fixed-size. Two sites (items 2 and 4) write to pointers or lengths whose provenance was not traced.

## 6 Most dangerous exposed capability

- Not established. The KS property and stream paths are the user surface; the two unresolved memcpy sites (items 2 and 4) are the open candidates.

## 7 Verdict

**NO-MEMORY-WRITE (PROVISIONAL).** No caller-controlled kernel write established in the traced sites; two memcpy sites remain open. The image is unsigned (NotSigned), so it is not loadable on a normally configured system. Reach R3 (devnode); Setup: hardware. HW-only: no.

## 8 Limitations

- Memcpy sites at dump 13520 (caller output pointer) and 12263-12312 (`_Dst` and `param_2` copies) are not traced to their KS caller. These decide the verdict; the verdict is provisional until they are resolved.
- The KS property and stream handlers were not traced end to end (the dump is about 677 KB image, many hundreds of functions).
- No REA session used. No execution, no driver load, no registry writes.
