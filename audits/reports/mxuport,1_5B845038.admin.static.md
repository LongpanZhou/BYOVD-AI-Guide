## 4 Access-control audit

- No `SeAccessCheck` or privilege check in the switch. Gates are device state and the length checks above.

## 5 Memory-write trace

- Every output write in the read cases is fixed-offset and gated by the output length (dump 777, 786, 792, 802, 820, 843, 851). Destinations are the SystemBuffer at fixed offsets (0..0x13). No caller-chosen index. Bounded.
- Input-to-state copies (0x1B005C, 0x1B0064) are fixed-size (4 or 16 bytes) into device state (dump 813-814, 834-835).
- The unread cases (0x1B0008-0x1B0044, 0x1B0080-0x1B009C) are the open candidates. This family is the same code as `deckser_65A92D8F` (read in full, no write primitive), but the sample here is partial.

## 6 Most dangerous exposed capability

- Not established for the read cases. The unread queue cases are the open candidates.

## 7 Verdict

**NO-MEMORY-WRITE (PROVISIONAL).** No caller-controlled kernel write in the read cases. The unread cases are open. Reach R3 (devnode / hardware); Setup: hardware; W0 in the read cases. The image is signed (Valid). HW-only: no.

## 8 Limitations

- The IOCTL switch was read for its output-handling cases only. The queue cases (0x1B0008-0x1B0044, 0x1B0080-0x1B009C) and the helper routines they call were not read.
- Device-creation logic (FUN_140001170) was read only at the IoCreateDevice and IoCreateSymbolicLink calls.
- No REA session used. No execution, no driver load, no registry writes.
