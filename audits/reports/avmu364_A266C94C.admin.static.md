## 1 Internals

- PE32+ x64 WDM / KS hybrid TV tuner driver (KS framework: `KsInitializeDriver`, `KsCreateFilterFactory`, `KsRegisterFilterWithNoKSPins`, `KsStreamPointer*`, `KsPinAttemptProcessing`).
- Imports include `IoRegisterDeviceInterface`, `IoGetDeviceInterfaces`, `IoOpenDeviceInterfaceRegistryKey`, `ZwCreateFile`, `ZwReadFile`, `ZwQueryInformationFile`, `ZwCreateKey`, `RtlWriteRegistryValue`, `PsCreateSystemThread`, `KeWaitForMultipleObjects`, `KeBugCheckEx`, `ExAllocatePoolWithTag`, `USBD_*` (three USBD parse/create calls).
- No MmMapIoSpace, MmMapLockedPagesSpecifyCache, MmCopyMemory, ZwMapViewOfSection, MmGetPhysicalAddress, MmGetSystemRoutineAddress, ProbeForWrite or RtlCopyMemory import. `memcpy`, `memmove` are not imported (CRT is internal).

## 2 Entry / device / interface / effective DACL

- Device creation is through KS / the WDM stack; the driver registers device interfaces with `IoRegisterDeviceInterface` and reads the device-interface registry key. No `IoCreateDeviceSecure`, no `ZwSetSecurityObject`, no SDDL string in the image. Effective DACL: default, not fixed in the image.

## 3 Dispatch and IOCTL table

- No custom IOCTL dispatch found: a grep of the dump for `== 0x22xxxx` and `== 0x2xxxxx` comparisons returns no IOCTL case labels. The request surface is KS (property, method, event and stream IRPs routed by the KS framework).
- Dispatch-slot stores are present (for example `param_1 + 0xE0` at dump 2165, 17935) but are KS/driver-internal state, not user-facing IOCTL routing (not traced further).

## 4 Access-control audit

- No privilege or caller-identity check in the driver. Access is governed by the KS device interface and the default device security (§2).

## 5 Memory-write trace

- No mapping, copy or pointer-arithmetic write through a user-supplied address was located by grep (see §3). Writes to driver structures (for example `param_1 + 0x70` and `+0xE0` counters at dump 15131-15987, 11483, 11870) are fixed-offset state updates.
- The bulk of the 94 pattern matches in the dump are internal state stores; they were not each classified.

## 6 Most dangerous exposed capability

- Not established. The KS stream and property paths are the user surface and were not traced.

## 7 Verdict

**NO-MEMORY-WRITE (PROVISIONAL).** No caller-controlled kernel write found in the traced code. The image is unsigned (NotSigned), so it is not loadable on a normally configured system; this does not change the W0 verdict. Reach R3 (devnode); Setup: hardware. HW-only: no.

## 8 Limitations

- The KS property/method/stream handlers are not traced. This is the user-reachable surface and is the largest part of the image (the dump is 2.9 MB; 246+ functions).
- The 94 pattern matches (IOCTL and copy helper names) were not individually classified.
- No REA session used. No execution, no driver load, no registry writes.
